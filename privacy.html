Privacy Policy

FieldLog — Coffey Construction LLC
Effective August 25, 2026

1. What FieldLog is

FieldLog is a private, internal business application built and operated by Coffey Construction LLC ("we", "us") to manage our own customers, jobs, work hours, expenses, and invoicing. It is not a product offered to the public. There is no public sign-up, no customer portal, and no way for anyone outside our company to create an account or access the system.

2. Who uses it

Access is limited to authorized Coffey Construction LLC personnel — currently two individuals — each authenticated with an individual password. Accounts are created manually by an administrator; self-registration is disabled.

3. What information the system holds

  • Business contact records for our customers: name, company, service address, phone number, email address, and job notes. This information is entered by us in the course of doing business, not collected from website visitors.
  • Job records: descriptions, status, work hours logged, and completion dates.
  • Financial records: expenses, vendor names, materials and equipment costs, invoices, payments, and receipts.
  • Uploaded documents: scanned invoices and receipts that predate this system.
  • Voice entries: short audio recordings or typed text used to log hours and expenses in the field.

We do not collect information from members of the public, and the application serves no advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

4. QuickBooks Online

FieldLog can connect to our own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's official OAuth 2.0 authorization, for one purpose: comparing expense transactions already recorded in QuickBooks against expenses recorded in FieldLog, so that a project expense we forgot to enter can be identified.

  • The connection is read-only. FieldLog never creates, edits, or deletes anything in QuickBooks.
  • It reads expense-type transactions (purchases and bills) within a date range we select: transaction date, amount, payee, account, and memo.
  • QuickBooks data is used only for this reconciliation comparison. It is not shared with anyone, sold, or used for any other purpose.
  • Authorization tokens issued by Intuit are stored encrypted at rest. The encryption key is held separately in the hosting environment and is never stored in the database or included in backups.
  • The connection can be revoked at any time from within FieldLog or from the Apps page of QuickBooks Online. Revoking it deletes the stored tokens.

5. Third-party services

FieldLog relies on the following providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Railway — application and database hosting (United States).
  • Intuit / QuickBooks Online — read-only accounting data, as described above.
  • Anthropic — interprets voice and text field entries into structured records.
  • OpenAI — optional transcription of voice recordings to text.
  • Google Drive — storage location for encrypted-in-transit database backups.

Voice entries may contain a customer's name or job address, and that text is transmitted to the providers above for interpretation. No other category of data is sent to them.

6. Storage, security, and retention

  • All access is over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.
  • Sessions use signed tokens that expire and can be revoked immediately.
  • QuickBooks authorization tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • Backups are taken nightly and retained on a rolling schedule (daily, weekly, monthly).
  • Financial records are retained as long as required for tax, accounting, and warranty purposes. Business records are not deleted on a fixed schedule.

No system is perfectly secure, and we make no guarantee that these measures are sufficient against every possible threat.

7. Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information in FieldLog. We do not disclose it to third parties except the service providers listed in section 5, our accountant, and where required by law.
